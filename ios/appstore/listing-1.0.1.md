# App Store listing for 1.0.1

Ready to paste into App Store Connect. The live 1.0 listing is in listing.md.

## How this was chosen

- Search check (US and Thai App Store, 20 Nov 2026):
  - In the US store the app ranks #22 for its own name, and isn't in the top 200 for
    "thai keyboard", "thai typing", "type thai" or "thai transliteration".
  - In the Thai store it ranks #1 for "karaoke thai keyboard", #8 "karaoke thai",
    #36 "type thai", #42 "thai keyboard".
  - Apple's autocomplete suggests "thai keyboard" and "karaoke thai keyboard", nothing
    for "romanized thai".
  - The top 10 for "thai keyboard" are mostly translator keyboards with 0–55 ratings, so
    a few dozen ratings would put this app near the top.
- Search words are counted once across the title, subtitle and keyword field. So no
  keyword repeats a title or subtitle word, there are no spaces after commas, and no
  plurals or the word "app".
- The description and promotional text are not searched; they are written to convince
  people.
- Screenshot captions are read by App Store search, so the first two carry the main
  phrases ("Thai keyboard", "type Thai", "English letters", "romanized").
- Ratings are the biggest ranking lever. 1.0.1 asks for one once, right after the third
  practice phrase comes out right.
- "keyboard translator" was left out: it has search volume, but people searching it want
  meaning translation, and a mismatch costs conversion.

## English (U.S.), primary

**Name (30 max, 21):** Karaoke Thai Keyboard

**Subtitle (30 max, 28):** Type Thai in English Letters

**Keywords (100 max, 97):**
phonetic,romanized,romanization,transliteration,typing,learn,language,input,chat,ภาษาไทย,พิมพ์ไทย

**Promotional text (170 max, 153, can change any time without review):**
Type Thai the way you text it: sawatdeekrap → สวัสดีครับ. Whole sentences, any spelling, typos fixed. Works in every app, fully offline. No subscription.

**Description:**
Type Thai by sound, using the English letters you already know. Most romanized Thai keyboards make you learn their spelling system first. Karaoke Thai doesn't: spell a word the way you would in a chat and it finds the Thai.

Type "sawatdeekrap" and pick สวัสดีครับ. Type "kinkhao" and get กินข้าว. Type a whole sentence without spaces and the keyboard splits it into words for you.

• Spell it your way: kap, krap, khrap and krub all find ครับ. kin or gin, khao or kao or kow, laew or leow or lew.
• Whole sentences: it reads the words around each word to pick the right one (kinkhao → กินข้าว, not กินเขา).
• Fixes typos: a swapped letter, a missed key or a slip in the middle of a sentence still finds the right Thai (sawatfeekrap → สวัสดีครับ).
• English too: misspell an English word and the correct spelling is offered as a second guess.
• Names and places: nicknames, provinces, countries and brands are built in.
• Learns from you: words you pick come first next time.
• Private: everything runs on your device. The keyboard never connects to the internet.
• Feels native: light and dark mode, iPhone and iPad layouts, keyboard colours, optional haptics.

For Thai speakers who type faster on an English layout, Thai kids growing up abroad, partners and expats in Thailand, and learners who know how a word sounds but not how to spell it.

One purchase, no subscription, no ads.

How to turn it on: Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → Karaoke Thai. "Allow Full Access" is optional and only enables key haptics.

**What's New in 1.0.1:**
Smarter with the way people really type:
• Typos fixed inside sentences: a slip of the finger no longer breaks the whole sentence (sawatfeekrap → สวัสดีครับ)
• Misspelled English words get the right spelling as the second guess
• More real-life spellings: "ahn" and "aan" for ัน (gahn = กัน), "ur" for อือ (kurn = ขึ้น), "e" for เออ (den = เดิน), "ew" for อิว (hew = หิว)
• Short words: ka = แค่, ma = แม่
• Food orders come out whole: kaopadkai = ข้าวผัดไก่, kapraomoo = กะเพราหมู
• Keyboard colours: tap the palette at the right of the bar
• Keys line up exactly like Apple's keyboard, and taps between keys always land
• New practice screen in the app

## Free copies for 20 people

Use App Store promo codes (App Store Connect → Promo Codes). Nothing in the app changes.
- Each code is one free download of the paid app, on the person's own Apple Account.
- 100 codes per version. A code expires 28 days after it is made, so make codes when
  you're ready to hand them out.
- Codes only work in countries where the app is on sale, and can't be sold or swapped
  for anything.
- To redeem: App Store → account picture → Redeem Gift Card or Code, or the link
  `https://apps.apple.com/redeem?code=CODE`.
- After the app turns free (1.1), a code for the unlock is an "offer code" on the
  in-app purchase instead.
