Karaoke Thai Keyboard: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Summary

Karaoke Thai Keyboard does not collect, store on any server, or share anything you type. The keyboard works entirely on your device and never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

To rank words you use often higher, the keyboard keeps a small list of the words you picked. This list is stored only on your device, is never transmitted, and is deleted when you delete the app.

For the free plan, the keyboard counts how many Thai words it typed today and remembers the day you started using it. It keeps these two numbers on your device and in your device's Keychain (so deleting and reinstalling the app doesn't restart the free week). They contain nothing you typed and are never transmitted.

Allow Full Access

iOS only lets a keyboard use the Keychain, the storage it shares with its app, and haptic feedback when Full Access is on. Karaoke Thai uses it for exactly these: to keep the free-plan record above, to see an unlock bought in the app, and for a light tap on each key. With or without Full Access, the keyboard makes no network connections and sends nothing you type anywhere.

In-app purchase

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple. The app and keyboard only ask iOS (Apple's StoreKit) whether the unlock was bought; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Data collection

None. There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no third-party services in the app. Apple may show us anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics (such as download counts) as it does for every app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
