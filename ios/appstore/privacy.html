Karaoke Thai Keyboard: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

Karaoke Thai Keyboard does not collect, store on any server, or share anything you type. The keyboard works entirely on your device and never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

To rank words you use often higher, the keyboard keeps a small list of the words you picked. This list is stored only on your device, is never transmitted, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Allow Full Access

Full Access is optional. iOS requires it for keyboard haptic feedback, and haptics are the only thing the keyboard uses it for. With or without Full Access, the keyboard makes no network connections and sends no data anywhere.

Data collection

None. There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no third-party services in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
