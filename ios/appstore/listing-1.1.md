## English (U.S.)

**Promotional text:**
Type Thai the way you text it: sawatdeekrap becomes สวัสดีครับ. Whole sentences, any spelling, typos fixed. Works in every app, fully offline. Now free to try.

**Description:**
Type Thai by sound, using the English letters you already know. Most romanized Thai keyboards make you learn their spelling system first. Karaoke Thai doesn't: spell a word the way you would in a chat and it finds the Thai.

Type "sawatdeekrap" and pick สวัสดีครับ. Type "kinkhao" and get กินข้าว. Type a whole sentence without spaces and the keyboard splits it into words for you.

• Spell it your way: kap, krap, khrap and krub all find ครับ. kin or gin, khao or kao or kow, laew or leow or lew.
• Whole sentences: it reads the words around each word to pick the right one (kinkhao → กินข้าว, not กินเขา).
• Fixes typos: a swapped letter, a missed key or a slip in the middle of a sentence still finds the right Thai (sawatfeekrap → สวัสดีครับ).
• English too: misspell an English word and the correct spelling is offered as a second guess.
• Names and places: nicknames, provinces, countries and brands are built in.
• Learns from you: words you pick come first next time.
• Private: everything runs on your device. The keyboard never connects to the internet.
• Feels native: light and dark mode, iPhone and iPad layouts, keyboard colours, optional haptics.

For Thai speakers who type faster on an English layout, Thai kids growing up abroad, partners and expats in Thailand, and learners who know how a word sounds but not how to spell it.

Free to download:
• 7 days with no limit (turn on Allow Full Access for the free week).
• After that, 20 Thai words a day, every day, free.
• One-time unlock for no limit, on all your devices. No subscription, no ads.
• Bought Karaoke Thai before it was free? It's already unlocked.

How to turn it on: Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → Karaoke Thai, then tap Karaoke Thai and turn on Allow Full Access. Full Access lets the keyboard keep your free week and see your unlock; what you type still never leaves your device.

## Notes for App Review (App Review Information → Notes)

Karaoke Thai is now free to download with a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase
("Unlock").

- Free plan: the keyboard is unlimited for the first 7 days, then converts 20 Thai words a
  day. After the daily words are used, typed letters stay as English and the bar explains
  how to unlock.
- The 7 free days need "Allow Full Access" (Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards →
  Karaoke Thai). iOS only gives a keyboard the Keychain with Full Access, and the free week
  is kept there so deleting and reinstalling doesn't start a new one. Without Full Access
  the 20-words-a-day plan applies from the start; the keyboard otherwise works the same.
- Full Access is also how the keyboard reads the unlock bought in the app. The keyboard
  never uses the network, with or without Full Access.
- Unlock, Restore Purchase and Redeem a Code are on the app's main screen, in the
  "Unlimited" section. Once the free week is over (or the keyboard used up the day's words),
  the app also shows an unlock screen when opened, at most every few days; "Not now" closes it.
- People who bought the app while it was paid stay unlocked (checked with
  AppTransaction.originalAppVersion).
- To try the limit quickly: leave Full Access off and type about 20 words in any text field.
