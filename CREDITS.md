# Credits

The code is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

The word data is derived from the sources below; only word lists, spellings and counts are included, never
the original texts.

- **[PyThaiNLP](https://github.com/PyThaiNLP/pythainlp)** (Apache-2.0; its word lists CC0-1.0):
  Thai word list, word segmentation, and the lists of provinces and countries.
- **Thai romanizer** (`thai2rom`, Apache-2.0; training data
  [thai-romanization](https://github.com/wannaphong/thai-romanization/) CC0-1.0): romanized spellings.
- **[Thai G2P](https://github.com/wannaphong/thai-g2p/)** by Wannaphong Phatthiyaphaibun
  ([CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)): pronunciations of the most common words.
- **[Thai Male and Female Names Corpus](https://github.com/korkeatw/thai-names-corpus/)** by Korkeat
  Wannapat ([CC-BY-SA-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), distributed with PyThaiNLP):
  first names and surnames in `web/names.js`. Under its ShareAlike terms that list stays freely
  reusable; CC-BY-SA-4.0 material may be included in GPL-3.0 work.
- **Thai National Corpus (TNC)** word, word-pair and three-word counts, as distributed with
  PyThaiNLP under CC0-1.0 (word counts compiled by Korakot Chaovavanich from the corpus by the
  Faculty of Arts, Chulalongkorn University): how often each word is used in formal writing.
- **[Wisesight Sentiment Corpus](https://github.com/PyThaiNLP/wisesight-sentiment)** (CC0):
  word counts from social-media posts.
- **[Wongnai Corpus](https://github.com/wongnai/wongnai-corpus)** (LGPL-3.0): word counts
  from restaurant reviews.

Hand-written spellings, chat words, phrases and names (`web/extra.js`,
`tools/names_hand.txt`) are part of this project.
